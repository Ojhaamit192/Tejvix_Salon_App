Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026
In short: to book a token, we only ask for your name and mobile number. We do not collect your email, address, payment details, location, or any other personal information. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

When you book a token or an appointment on this platform, we collect only:

  • Your name — so the salon knows who to call for their turn.
  • Your mobile number — so we can send you an SMS with your token number, queue updates, and (after your visit) a link to rate your experience.

If you choose to leave a rating or comment after a visit, we also store that rating and comment text.

What we don't collect

  • No email address, home address, or date of birth.
  • No payment or card details — this platform doesn't process any payments.
  • No GPS or precise location tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or anything else on your device.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your token or appointment in the queue.
  • To send you SMS notifications about your booking, your turn, and a post-visit review request, through our SMS provider, Fast2SMS.
  • To calculate simple, aggregated statistics for the salon owner (like "how many customers today"), which do not identify you individually.
  • To recognize repeat visits under the same mobile number, so salons can offer loyalty rewards.

Who can see your information

Your name and phone number are visible to the staff of the specific salon you booked with, so they can call you for your turn. They are not visible to other customers or to other salons on this platform. We share your phone number with our SMS delivery partner (Fast2SMS) solely to send you the messages described above.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept so salons can track daily queues and repeat visits for loyalty rewards. You can ask the salon or platform to delete your data at any time (see Contact below).

Your choices

  • You can ask a salon to delete your booking history at any time.
  • You can choose not to submit a post-visit rating — it's entirely optional.
  • You can simply stop using the service at any time; nothing is required to "opt out."

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach out to the salon you booked with directly, or contact the platform at info@tejvix.com.
