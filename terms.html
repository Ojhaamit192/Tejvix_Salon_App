What this platform is

This is a token and queue management tool that helps local salons manage walk-ins and appointments, and lets customers see live wait times and get SMS updates. Each salon listed here operates independently — the platform simply provides the booking and queue technology.

Booking a token or appointment

  • A token number reserves your place in that salon's queue for the day; it does not guarantee an exact time, since actual service time can vary.
  • Estimated wait times are approximate, based on average service duration, and may change as the queue moves.
  • For pre-booked appointments, please arrive on time. If you don't check in within a reasonable window of your slot, the salon may treat it as a no-show and offer the slot to someone else.
  • If you're called for your turn and don't respond or arrive, staff may mark you as a no-show and move to the next customer.

SMS notifications

By providing your mobile number, you agree to receive SMS messages related to your booking — confirmation, queue updates, your turn, and an optional post-visit review request. These messages are limited to your booking; we don't send promotional messages unrelated to your visit.
